## Floci (free hands-on practice, no AWS account needed)

- Repo: https://github.com/floci-io/floci
- Docs: https://floci-io.github.io/
- Why we use it: MIT-licensed, no auth token, no feature gates, fast startup, backs
  RDS/ECS/EKS/EC2 with real Docker containers — closer to real AWS behavior than most
  "local emulator" tools, and completely free.
